The #RMDA (Redefining Mechanics, Dynamics, and Aesthetics) framework stands as a transformative approach to game design methodology, particularly tailored to address challenges posed by domains where creativity is paramount, such as music, movies, literature, and games. Embracing a structured methodology, #RMDA integrates a clear ontology to harmonize structured design with the inherent need for creativity in game development. By redefining the foundational #MDA concepts and incorporating elements like Creativity Support, Connecting Aesthetics Objectives, and the integration of the 6-11 framework, #RMDA offers a nuanced and comprehensive guide for game designers, fostering a balance between creativity and methodological rigor to enhance the quality of the development process.

## 1. Objective and Context:

### 1.1 Objective:

- #### Structured Design Methodology:
  - Overcoming challenges in acceptance of game design methodologies, especially in creative domains.
  - #RMDA addresses the common resistance to structured design methodologies, prevalent in creative domains like music, movies, literature, and games. The aim is to bridge the gap between creativity and structure, making the adoption of a structured methodology more feasible for game designers.

- #### Balance of Creativity and Structure:
  - Recognizing tension between creativity and structure, proposing a methodology without stifling creativity.
  - #RMDA acknowledges the delicate balance required in game design between fostering creativity and providing a structured framework. The objective is to present a methodology that doesn't impede creative expression but, instead, enhances it by providing a clear structure supported by ontology.

- #### Connection to Aesthetics Objectives:
  - Justifying design decisions by connecting them to aesthetics objectives and emotional responses.
  - #RMDA seeks to establish a profound connection between design decisions and aesthetics objectives. By linking aesthetics considerations to all abstraction layers of a game, it aims to provide a rationale for decisions, whether they involve subtle elements like camera mechanics or fundamental changes to core game mechanics.

- #### Guidance for Designers:
  - Serving as a guide for aligning creations with intended emotional and aesthetic outcomes.
  - #RMDA positions itself as a guiding tool for designers throughout the creative process. By offering support and direction, it helps designers align their creations with the intended emotional responses and aesthetic goals, enhancing the overall quality of the game development process.

### 1.2 Context:

- #### Challenges in Game Design:
  - Acknowledging the complexity and unique characteristics of game design.
  - #RMDA operates within the context of the intricate and multifaceted nature of game design. It recognizes the challenges specific to this domain, including the need for methodologies that can accommodate the diverse and creative aspects inherent in game development.

- #### #MDA Framework Redefinition (#RMDA):
  - Building upon #MDA, addressing limitations, and introducing #RMDA for wider applicability.
  - #RMDA doesn't exist in isolation; it evolves from the #MDA framework. It acknowledges the strengths and limitations of #MDA, proposing a redefined version (#RMDA) to overcome the identified shortcomings. This redefinition is aimed at making the framework more versatile and applicable to various game design scenarios.

- #### Connection to Aesthetics and Emotions:
  - Emphasizing connecting aesthetics objectives to all abstraction layers for justified design decisions.
  - #RMDA is contextualized in the importance of aesthetics and emotions in game design. It emphasizes the need to establish a profound connection between aesthetics objectives and all levels of abstraction in a game. This connection serves as a foundation for justifying design decisions based on the intended emotional impact.

- #### Use in Game Development:
  - Currently used in practical game development, envisioning future use in game design studies.
  - #RMDA is not merely a theoretical construct; it is actively employed in practical game development. Additionally, #RMDA envisions broader adoption in the future, potentially as an educational tool in game design studies.

The #RMDA framework, therefore, is positioned as a response to the challenges inherent in game design, offering a balanced and structured approach that aligns with the creative nature of the domain. It builds upon the foundations of #MDA, aiming for wider applicability and practical use in both current game development and future educational contexts.

## 5. 6-11 Framework Integration

### 5.1 Background

- #### Objective Enhancement
  - The primary aim is to enhance the understanding of player emotions and subjective experiences within the realm of game design, emphasizing the importance of player-centric design.
  - This objective contextualizes the significance of player emotions as a crucial aspect of game design, setting the stage for the integration of the 6-11 framework.

- #### The 6-11 Framework
  - References Roberto Dillon's 6-11 framework, which centers on six emotions and eleven instincts, laying the foundation for a structured approach to analyzing player reactions.
  - Establishes the existing framework as a basis for further integration into #RMDA, acknowledging its role in capturing essential elements of player experience.

### 5.2 Integration in #RMDA

- #### Expanded Modeling of Emotions
  - Aims to expand the emotional modeling aspect within #RMDA by integrating the 6-11 framework, offering a more nuanced understanding of player emotions.
  - Recognizes the need for a detailed emotional model that goes beyond traditional approaches, aligning with the overarching objectives of #RMDA.

- #### Comprehensive Emotional Objectives
  - **Objective:** Augments emotional objectives within #RMDA to encompass a broader spectrum of emotions, providing designers with a comprehensive palette to evoke varied player responses.
  - **Context:** Emphasizes the need for a holistic approach to emotional objectives, aligning with #RMDA's goal of connecting aesthetics to all abstraction layers.

- #### Support for Designers
  - **Objective:** Acts as an additional layer for designers to consider, offering a structured framework for incorporating specific emotional responses into the game design process.
  - **Context:** Positions the integration as a practical tool for designers, enhancing their ability to craft emotionally resonant experiences for players.

### 5.3 Future Work

- #### Opportunity for Further Exploration
  - **Objective:** Recognizes the integration of emotional frameworks as an evolving area, signaling the potential for additional research and refinement in understanding player emotions.
  - **Context:** Acknowledges the dynamic nature of game design and the continuous evolution of methodologies, leaving room for future investigations and improvements.

- #### Complementary Methodologies
  - **Objective:** Encourages the combination of integrated emotional frameworks with other methodologies, fostering a holistic approach to game design.
  - **Context:** Advocates for synergy between different methodologies, recognizing their collective potential in addressing the multifaceted aspects of game development.

The integration of the 6-11 framework into #RMDA enriches the emotional landscape of game design, offering a nuanced perspective on player experiences. This enhancement aligns with #RMDA's overarching objectives, emphasizing the continuous evolution and complementary nature of methodologies within the game design domain.

## Implementing #RMDA:

Implementing the #RMDA framework in a game design project involves several actionable steps:

### Understand the #RMDA Framework:
- [ ] Familiarize yourself with the principles and components of the #RMDA framework.
- [ ] Engage in discussions or forums to gain insights into practical applications of #RMDA.

### Identify Project Objectives:
- [ ] Conduct a stakeholder meeting to gather diverse perspectives on project objectives.
- [ ] Prioritize objectives based on impact and feasibility.

### Create an Ontology:
- [ ] Collaborate with domain experts to create a comprehensive game ontology.
- [ ] Regularly update the ontology to accommodate evolving project needs.

### Connect Aesthetics Objectives:
- [ ] Host workshops with the development team to brainstorm connections between aesthetics and gameplay.
- [ ] Map out the aesthetics objectives on a visual representation for clarity.

### Utilize the 6-11 Framework:
- [ ] Conduct training sessions to educate team members on the 6-11 framework.
- [ ] Establish a feedback loop for continuous improvement of emotional modeling.

### Consider Objective and Context:
- [ ] Conduct a SWOT analysis to understand the project's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- [ ] Clearly communicate the project's context to team members through documentation.

### Explore Complementary Methodologies:
- [ ] Research emerging game design methodologies and assess their compatibility with #RMDA.
- [ ] Pilot the integration of a complementary methodology in a small project.

### Iterative Design Process:
- [ ] Implement a version control system to track changes in design iterations.
- [ ] Conduct regular retrospectives to reflect on the effectiveness of design iterations.

### Evaluate Experiences:
- [ ] Develop key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with #RMDA objectives.

### Document and Communicate:

### Training and Education:

### Continuous Improvement:

### Share Insights:

- [ ] Organize webinars or workshops to share #RMDA insights with the broader game development community.

By incorporating these additional items into the implementation plan, you create a more comprehensive and robust approach to applying the #RMDA framework in game design projects. Each item contributes to a holistic understanding and effective utilization of #RMDA principles.
